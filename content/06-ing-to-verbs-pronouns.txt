# 51 | work/working, go/going, do/doing (verb forms after other verbs) | -ing and to… | A2
summary: Which verb form comes next? Base verb after modals (can go), to + verb after want/need (want to go), -ing after be/enjoy/stop (is going, enjoy going).
[learn]
## 1. Base verb (no to)
After **modals**: *will, can, could, must, might, should, would, shall, may*, and after **do/does/did**.
= I **can swim**.
= You **should go** home.
= She **didn't call**.
= **Do** you **know** him?

## 2. to + base verb
After verbs like **want, need, hope, decide, plan, try, learn, would like, have to, be going to**:
= I **want to go** home.
= We **need to talk**.
= She **decided to stay**.
= I'm **going to buy** a car.

## 3. -ing
After **be** (continuous tenses) and verbs like **enjoy, stop, finish, mind, like/love/hate** (often), and after **prepositions**:
= I **am working**.
= She **enjoys cooking**.
= **Stop talking**!
= I'm good **at drawing**.

## 4. Past participle (done, seen, worked)
After **have** (perfect) and **be** (passive):
= I **have finished**.
= It **was made** in Japan.

## Summary
| After… | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| can / will / should / did | base | can **go** |
| want / need / decide | to + base | want **to go** |
| be (continuous) / enjoy / stop | -ing | is **going**, enjoy **going** |
| have (perfect) / be (passive) | participle | have **gone**, was **built** |
[examples]
I can't come tomorrow.
Do you want to dance?
She's watching the news.
I hope to see you soon.
We've finished eating.
They stopped talking when I came in.
You must try this curry.
The bridge was built in 1880.
[mistakes]
I can to swim. => I can swim. :: Modal + base verb.
I want go home. => I want to go home. :: want + to + verb.
I enjoy to read. => I enjoy reading. :: enjoy + -ing.

[medium]

## Subject and object pronouns
| Subject | Object |
|---|---|
| I | **me** |
| you | **you** |
| he | **him** |
| she | **her** |
| it | **it** |
| we | **us** |
| they | **them** |

## Subject = before the verb (who does the action)
= **I** like tea.
= **She** called yesterday.
= **They** live in Pune.

= My mother loves **me**.
= I called **her** yesterday.

They told us their news.

You have your opinion and I have mine.

Me give him mine book. => I give him my book. :: subject I; my + noun.

They invited we to their party. => They invited us to their party. :: object → us

[learn]
## Forms
| | |
|---|---|
| I → **myself** | we → **ourselves** |
| you → **yourself** (one person) | you → **yourselves** (more than one) |

= I cut **myself** with a knife.

| | |

= This is **Kate's** camera.
